Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Oak Lawn Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan service around your operating hours.

Hot water temperature is a critical concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Oak Lawn Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

Each hour without hot water costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. In a salon, it empties the chairs. For a hotel, it brings refund requests, and in an apartment building it leads to tenant disputes. Oak Lawn Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.
